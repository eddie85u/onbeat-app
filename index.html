Questions

Getting started

Is it free?

Yes. It is free, with no ads and no paid version. Donations are voluntary; US$10 (40,000 pesos) or more unlocks one cloud profile, kept in step on up to 3 devices at a time.

Is it for complete beginners?

Yes. The lessons start from scratch: the first one is the snare and the quarter note, and each lesson adds one thing. There is no teacher and no account; you go at your own pace.

Do I need to read music?

No. The app teaches you to read drum notation as you play: each line or space is one drum, a cross is a cymbal, and a moving line shows where you are.

Can I learn drums without a drum kit?

Yes. You can practise on a practice pad, on acoustic drums or on the computer keyboard. An electronic kit plugged in with a USB cable adds a check of every hit.

I already play. Where do I start?

Choose "I've played before" when the app opens. With the keyboard or an electronic kit, a placement run of about five minutes finds your level on each path. On a practice pad you pick your starting point on the map.

Which styles can I learn?

Rock, metal, pop and funk, Latin and jazz basics. The Latin path starts with Colombian rhythms: cumbia, vallenato and dembow. You choose one style, several, or the instrument itself, and can change it any time in Settings.

Your setup

Can it hear me on a practice pad?

No. On a pad or acoustic drums the app plays the exercise with you and you say whether each run was clean. With the keyboard or an electronic kit it checks every hit: on time, the right drum, loud or soft.

Which browser do I need?

On a computer, Google Chrome on Mac or Windows; it is the browser that can talk to an electronic kit. On a practice pad, the browser on a phone, tablet or TV works too.

Does it work on my TV?

If your TV has a web browser, yes, for practising on a pad. The app has a layout for big screens, a screensaver when nothing is playing, and a Picture setting in case the moving line runs ahead of the sound.

Is it in Spanish?

Yes. The app and this page are in English and Spanish (Colombia). Switch with EN | ES at the top at any time.

The desktop app

Where are the songs?

Playing along to songs is in the desktop app, for Mac or Windows. The app comes with no songs: you add a song's drum chart, made by fans for Clone Hero or YARG, and its audio if you have it.

How do I open the Mac app the first time?

Download Onbeat-mac.zip, open it and drag the app into Applications. The first time, macOS says it cannot check the app, because it is not from the App Store: click Done, then in System Settings > Privacy & Security click Open Anyway next to the message about the app.

Is there a Windows app?

Yes, for Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), with songs and a daily backup like the Mac app. Download it under Start practicing.

How do I open the Windows app the first time?

Download Onbeat-windows.zip, right-click it, choose Extract All, then open Onbeat.exe in the extracted folder. The first time, Windows says it protected your PC, because the app is not from the Microsoft Store: click More info, then Run anyway. It asks only once.

Your progress

Where is my progress saved?

On your device: in the browser for the web version, and in the app on a Mac or Windows computer. Settings, Backup saves it to a file you can move to another computer. Several people can share a device, each with a profile of their own.

How do I get the cloud profile?

Donate US$10 (40,000 pesos) or more and write to eddie@strategyandstack.com with your transfer details. You get a code to type in Settings > Sync, then you link your other devices with a QR code. Keep the code: it gets you back in on a new device. Only that profile's progress and settings are kept online, with no names or emails.

Help and about

Something not working, or have an idea?

Tell us on GitHub, or write to eddie@strategyandstack.com. Say what you were doing and on which device; a screenshot helps.
