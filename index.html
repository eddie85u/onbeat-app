Play on what you have

No drum kit yet? That is fine. Onbeat works with three things, and you can change at any time in Settings.

  • Practice pad

    A pad or acoustic drums

    Press Listen and the exercise plays over and over, drums and all, while you play along. Say whether it was clean, and the tempo goes up after three clean runs.

  • Keyboard

    The computer keyboard

    The keys are a drum kit: Space is the kick, F and J the snare. Every hit is checked for timing, so you can start reading before you own anything.

  • Electronic kit

    An electronic drum kit

    Plug the drum module into the computer with a USB cable. Onbeat finds it and checks every hit: on time, the right drum, loud or soft.

Lessons that grow with you

Short lessons in ten paths, from your first quarter notes to double kick and odd time. Each day, Today plans a session of 10, 20 or 45 minutes: a warm-up, something new, and a review.

  • Reading
  • Time and rhythm
  • Hands and rudiments
  • Grooves
  • Fills
  • Dynamics and ghost notes
  • Hi-hat
  • Independence
  • Double kick
  • Odd time

Easier, Harder

On a beat that is too hard, Easier takes one thing out. When it feels easy, Harder adds one thing, like a kick on the "and" of 3. One small step at a time.

TV, phone or computer

Put Onbeat on the TV and practise in the living room, or prop the phone on a music stand. The screen is laid out for each size.

  • On a big screen the music fills the width, with the kit picture and the buttons below it.
  • A screensaver comes on after three minutes with nothing playing.
  • If the moving line runs ahead of the sound, as it often does on a TV, a Picture setting lines them up.
  • Several people can share one device: each profile keeps its own progress, and Onbeat asks "Who's playing?" when it opens.

Songs, in the Mac app

In the Mac app you can play along to real songs, the whole song or one part at a time, with the same moving line and the same checks.

Onbeat comes with no songs. A song is a folder with a drum chart written by fans (charts made for Clone Hero and YARG work) and the song's audio if you have it. Drag the folder onto the Songs screen.

Your profile in the cloud

Practise on the TV tonight and carry on from the phone tomorrow. Donations are voluntary and any amount is welcome. Mac

For Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer). Includes songs.

  1. Download Onbeat-mac.zip, open it, and drag Onbeat into Applications.
  2. Open it. The first time, macOS says it cannot check the app, because it is not from the App Store. Click Done.
  3. In System Settings > Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway next to the message about Onbeat, and confirm.

Questions

Is Onbeat free?

Yes. Onbeat is free, with no ads and no paid version. Donations are voluntary and any amount is welcome; a donation of US$10 (40,000 pesos) or more unlocks one cloud profile, kept in step on up to 3 devices.

Do I need an electronic drum kit?

No. You can practise on a practice pad, on acoustic drums or on the computer keyboard. An electronic kit plugged in with a USB cable adds a check of every hit.

Can Onbeat hear me on a practice pad?

No. On a pad or acoustic drums Onbeat cannot hear you, so it plays the exercise with you and you say whether each run was clean. With the keyboard or an electronic kit it checks every hit: on time, the right drum, loud or soft.

Which browser do I need?

On a computer, use Google Chrome on Mac or Windows; it is the browser that can talk to an electronic kit. On a practice pad, the browser on a phone, tablet or TV works too.

Does it work on my TV?

If your TV has a web browser, yes, for practising on a pad. Onbeat has a layout for big screens, a screensaver when nothing is playing, and a Picture setting in case the moving line runs ahead of the sound, which is common on TVs.

Where are the songs?

Playing along to songs is in the Mac app. Onbeat comes with no songs: you add a song's drum chart, made by fans for Clone Hero or YARG, and its audio if you have it.

Is Onbeat in Spanish?

Yes. The app and this page are in English and Spanish (Colombia). Switch with EN | ES at the top at any time.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device: in the browser for the web version, and in the app on the Mac. Progress, Backup saves it to a file you can move to another computer. Supporters can unlock a cloud profile instead, saved online and kept in step on up to 3 devices.

Is there a Windows app?

Not yet; it is on the plan. Until then, the web version works in Chrome on Windows.
